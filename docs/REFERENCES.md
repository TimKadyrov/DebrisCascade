# References

The papers the model is built on and checked against. They are not redistributed here; follow the
links (some need a journal subscription).

## Models and methods

- Johnson, N. L., Krisko, P. H., Liou, J.-C., Anz-Meador, P. D. (2001). NASA's new breakup model of
  EVOLVE 4.0. *Advances in Space Research* 28(9), 1377–1384.
  [doi:10.1016/S0273-1177(01)00423-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0273-1177(01)00423-9)
  — the NASA Standard Breakup Model: fragment counts, area-to-mass ratios, the 40 J/g threshold.
- Krisko, P. H. (2011). Proper implementation of the 1998 NASA breakup model. *Orbital Debris
  Quarterly News* 15(4). [PDF (NASA ODPO)](https://www.orbitaldebris.jsc.nasa.gov/newsletter/pdfs/odqnv15i4.pdf)
  — the non-catastrophic mass term M = Mp·v².
- Liou, J.-C., Kessler, D. J., Matney, M., Stansbery, G. (2003). A new approach to evaluate collision
  probabilities among asteroids, comets, and Kuiper belt objects. *Lunar and Planetary Science
  XXXIV*, abstract 1828. [PDF (LPI)](https://www.lpi.usra.edu/meetings/lpsc2003/pdf/1828.pdf)
  — the cube method used by the conjunction engine.

## Debris-environment studies

- Kessler, D. J., Cour-Palais, B. G. (1978). Collision frequency of artificial satellites: the
  creation of a debris belt. *Journal of Geophysical Research* 83(A6), 2637–2646.
  [doi:10.1029/JA083iA06p02637](https://doi.org/10.1029/JA083iA06p02637)
- Liou, J.-C., Johnson, N. L. (2006). Risks in space from orbiting debris. *Science* 311(5759),
  340–341. [doi:10.1126/science.1121337](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1121337)
  — LEGEND with no new launches from the 2006 catalog: the benchmark in `--benchmark-2006`.
- Liou, J.-C., Johnson, N. L., Hill, N. M. (2010). Controlling the growth of future LEO debris
  populations with active debris removal. *Acta Astronautica* 66(5–6), 648–653.
  [doi:10.1016/j.actaastro.2009.08.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actaastro.2009.08.005)
- Liou, J.-C. (2011). An active debris removal parametric study for LEO environment remediation.
  *Advances in Space Research* 47(11), 1865–1876.
  [doi:10.1016/j.asr.2011.02.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.asr.2011.02.003)
- Liou, J.-C., et al. (2013). Stability of the future LEO environment — an IADC comparison study.
  *6th European Conference on Space Debris*, ESA SP-723.
  [PDF (ESA)](https://conference.sdo.esoc.esa.int/proceedings/sdc6/paper/199/sdc6-paper199.pdf)
